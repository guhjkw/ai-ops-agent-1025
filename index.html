    How it works

    Four steps, one of them yours.

    01

    It reads

    Email, website forms and attachments, the moment they arrive, from your inbox and your site.

    02

    It understands

    What kind of message it is, how sure it is, and the details that matter: company, budget, invoice number, dates.

    03

    It acts or asks

    Updates the CRM, helpdesk, accounting and calendar, or puts the decision in your queue with its reasoning.

    04 · You

    You set the rules

    Your limits, your people and your tone. Approve or reject from the queue, Slack or email.

    Under the hood

    One engine. Tested.
